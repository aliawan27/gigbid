# Case study: SaaS subscription billing

Client industry: SaaS
Year: 2021
Skills: Next.js, Stripe, PostgreSQL, TypeScript

## Problem
The client in the SaaS sector needed subscription billing with metered usage and dunning.

## What I did
I designed and built subscription billing with metered usage and dunning using Next.js, Stripe, PostgreSQL and TypeScript. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project recovered 12% of failed payments.
