# Case study: Patient portal with SSO

Client industry: healthcare
Year: 2022
Skills: React, Keycloak, SAML

## Problem
The client in the healthcare sector needed a patient portal with single sign-on for 4 clinics.

## What I did
I designed and built a patient portal with single sign-on for 4 clinics using React, Keycloak and SAML. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project passed an external security audit first time.
