# Case study: Realtime chat service

Client industry: social
Year: 2024
Skills: WebSockets, Redis, Go

## Problem
The client in the social sector needed a horizontally scaled chat backend.

## What I did
I designed and built a horizontally scaled chat backend using WebSockets, Redis and Go. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project served 40k concurrent connections.
