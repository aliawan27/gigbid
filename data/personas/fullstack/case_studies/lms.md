# Case study: Learning management system

Client industry: education
Year: 2022
Skills: Django, PostgreSQL, Celery, AWS S3

## Problem
The client in the education sector needed an LMS with video courses, quizzes and certificates.

## What I did
I designed and built an LMS with video courses, quizzes and certificates using Django, PostgreSQL, Celery and AWS S3. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project onboarded 3,000 students in the first term.
