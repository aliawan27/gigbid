# Case study: Hotel booking platform

Client industry: hospitality
Year: 2022
Skills: React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB

## Problem
The client in the hospitality sector needed a booking engine with live availability for 80 properties.

## What I did
I designed and built a booking engine with live availability for 80 properties using React, Node.js, Express and MongoDB. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project handled 25k bookings in the first season.
