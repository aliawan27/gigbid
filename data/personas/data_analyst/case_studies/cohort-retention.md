# Case study: Cohort retention analysis

Client industry: mobile apps
Year: 2023
Skills: SQL, Looker, cohort analysis

## Problem
The client in the mobile apps sector needed retention and LTV cohorts for a subscription app.

## What I did
I designed and built retention and LTV cohorts for a subscription app using SQL, Looker and cohort analysis. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project identified the onboarding step losing 30% of users.
