# Case study: Marketing attribution analysis

Client industry: e-commerce
Year: 2022
Skills: Python, pandas, SQL, Google Analytics 4

## Problem
The client in the e-commerce sector needed a multi-touch attribution model across 6 channels.

## What I did
I designed and built a multi-touch attribution model across 6 channels using Python, pandas, SQL and Google Analytics 4. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project reallocated 18% of ad spend.
