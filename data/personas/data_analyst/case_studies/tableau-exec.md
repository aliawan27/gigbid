# Case study: Hospital operations dashboards

Client industry: healthcare
Year: 2021
Skills: Tableau, SQL, Excel

## Problem
The client in the healthcare sector needed bed-occupancy and wait-time dashboards.

## What I did
I designed and built bed-occupancy and wait-time dashboards using Tableau, SQL and Excel. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project reduced ER wait-time reporting lag to daily.
