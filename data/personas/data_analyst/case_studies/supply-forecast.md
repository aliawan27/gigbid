# Case study: CPG supply forecast

Client industry: CPG
Year: 2024
Skills: time series, Excel, Python

## Problem
The client in the CPG sector needed monthly supply forecasts for 120 products.

## What I did
I designed and built monthly supply forecasts for 120 products using time series, Excel and Python. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project reduced overstock by 15%.
