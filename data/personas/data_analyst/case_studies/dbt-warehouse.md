# Case study: dbt analytics warehouse

Client industry: SaaS
Year: 2023
Skills: dbt, Snowflake, SQL

## Problem
The client in the SaaS sector needed a modelled warehouse with 60 tested dbt models.

## What I did
I designed and built a modelled warehouse with 60 tested dbt models using dbt, Snowflake and SQL. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.
