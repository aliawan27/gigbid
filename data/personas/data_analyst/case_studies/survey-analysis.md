# Case study: Donor survey analysis

Client industry: nonprofit
Year: 2024
Skills: R, statistics, ggplot2

## Problem
The client in the nonprofit sector needed analysis of a 5,000-response donor survey.

## What I did
I designed and built analysis of a 5,000-response donor survey using R, statistics and ggplot2. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project informed a campaign that grew donations 12%.
