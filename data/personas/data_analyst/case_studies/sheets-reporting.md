# Case study: Agency client reporting

Client industry: agency
Year: 2023
Skills: Google Sheets, Apps Script, Looker Studio

## Problem
The client in the agency sector needed automated client reports for a marketing agency.

## What I did
I designed and built automated client reports for a marketing agency using Google Sheets, Apps Script and Looker Studio. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project cut reporting time per client from 3h to 20 min.
