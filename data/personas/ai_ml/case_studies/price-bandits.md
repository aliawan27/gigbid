# Case study: Dynamic pricing with bandits

Client industry: travel
Year: 2023
Skills: reinforcement learning, Python, multi-armed bandits

## Problem
The client in the travel sector needed a contextual-bandit price tester for hotel rooms.

## What I did
I designed and built a contextual-bandit price tester for hotel rooms using reinforcement learning, Python and multi-armed bandits. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project added 4.1% revenue per room.
