# Case study: Retail demand forecasting

Client industry: retail
Year: 2024
Skills: time series, LightGBM, Prophet, Airflow

## Problem
The client in the retail sector needed SKU-level weekly demand forecasts for 300 stores.

## What I did
I designed and built SKU-level weekly demand forecasts for 300 stores using time series, LightGBM, Prophet and Airflow. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project reduced MAPE from 18% to 11%.
