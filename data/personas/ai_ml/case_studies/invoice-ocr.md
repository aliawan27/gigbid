# Case study: Invoice OCR extraction

Client industry: fintech
Year: 2023
Skills: PyTorch, OCR, LayoutLM, Docker

## Problem
The client in the fintech sector needed an invoice field-extraction pipeline for 40 vendor layouts.

## What I did
I designed and built an invoice field-extraction pipeline for 40 vendor layouts using PyTorch, OCR, LayoutLM and Docker. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project cut manual entry time by 70%.
