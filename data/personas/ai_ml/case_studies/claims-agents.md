# Case study: Insurance claims multi-agent workflow

Client industry: insurance
Year: 2025
Skills: LangGraph, multi-agent systems, tool calling, FastAPI

## Problem
The client in the insurance sector needed a multi-agent triage workflow for insurance claims with human review.

## What I did
I designed and built a multi-agent triage workflow for insurance claims with human review using LangGraph, multi-agent systems, tool calling and FastAPI. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project reduced triage time by 55%.
