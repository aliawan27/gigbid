# Case study: Telecom churn prediction

Client industry: telecom
Year: 2021
Skills: Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, feature engineering

## Problem
The client in the telecom sector needed a churn model over 2M subscriber records with SHAP explanations.

## What I did
I designed and built a churn model over 2M subscriber records with SHAP explanations using Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost and feature engineering. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project lifted recall from 0.61 to 0.78.
