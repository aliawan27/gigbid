# Case study: MLOps platform

Client industry: logistics
Year: 2021
Skills: MLflow, Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD

## Problem
The client in the logistics sector needed model training and deployment pipelines for 12 models.

## What I did
I designed and built model training and deployment pipelines for 12 models using MLflow, Kubernetes, Docker and CI/CD. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project cut release time from 2 weeks to 1 day.
