# Case study: Graph-based fraud detection

Client industry: fintech
Year: 2024
Skills: graph neural networks, PyTorch Geometric, Neo4j

## Problem
The client in the fintech sector needed a GNN over transaction graphs to flag mule accounts.

## What I did
I designed and built a GNN over transaction graphs to flag mule accounts using graph neural networks, PyTorch Geometric and Neo4j. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project caught 23% more fraud at the same alert volume.
