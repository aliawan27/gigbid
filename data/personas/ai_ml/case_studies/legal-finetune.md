# Case study: Legal document classifier

Client industry: legal
Year: 2024
Skills: fine-tuning, LoRA, Llama, PEFT

## Problem
The client in the legal sector needed a LoRA fine-tuned open model classifying contract clauses.

## What I did
I designed and built a LoRA fine-tuned open model classifying contract clauses using fine-tuning, LoRA, Llama and PEFT. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project matched GPT-4 accuracy at 1/20th the cost.
