# Case study: Review sentiment analytics

Client industry: marketing
Year: 2022
Skills: NLP, Hugging Face, sentiment analysis, Streamlit

## Problem
The client in the marketing sector needed aspect-level sentiment over 400k product reviews.

## What I did
I designed and built aspect-level sentiment over 400k product reviews using NLP, Hugging Face, sentiment analysis and Streamlit. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project surfaced 3 product issues adopted by the client.
