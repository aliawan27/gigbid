# Case study: Visual defect detection

Client industry: manufacturing
Year: 2021
Skills: computer vision, YOLOv8, OpenCV, PyTorch

## Problem
The client in the manufacturing sector needed a real-time defect detector on an edge device at 30 FPS.

## What I did
I designed and built a real-time defect detector on an edge device at 30 FPS using computer vision, YOLOv8, OpenCV and PyTorch. I worked directly with the client's team, wrote documentation and handed over tests.

## Outcome
The project reached 98.2% defect recall.
